About this site

This site exists for one simple reason: so that anyone, anywhere can have a free Bible and hear the Gospel of Jesus Christ. No cost, no account, no catch — just open the page and read.

If you found your way here by scanning a code, clicking a link, or hearing about it from a friend, you are welcome here. Everything on this site was made to be given away.

What is here

  • The complete World English Bible — the whole Bible, free to read right in your browser, with the words of Jesus in red.
  • The Gospel — a plain, friendly presentation of who Jesus is and what He has done for you.
  • Devotional pages — gentle words for hard nights and for hurting people, for whenever you need them.
  • Everyday tools and simple games — handy little helpers for daily life, and a bit of quiet fun.

All of it is free, and all of it always will be.

Who made it

This site was built by Tyler, a Christian missionary in eastern North Carolina who gives away Bibles in his town, together with Noki, his AI assistant. It is nothing fancy — just one man sharing what he himself has been given, and glad to share it with you.

Free means free

Everything here is public domain. Copy it, share it, print it, hand it to a friend — no permission needed, no strings attached. That is the whole point. Jesus said it best:

“Freely you received, so freely give.”

— Matthew 10:8, World English Bible
